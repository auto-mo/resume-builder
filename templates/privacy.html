Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 24, 2026

Resume Builder turns a job description into a tailored one-page resume and an optional cover letter. This policy explains what the hosted version at apps.mohithgujjula.com/resume does with the information you provide. The tool is also open source, so you can run your own private copy from GitHub.

What you provide

  • Resume files. The .docx resumes you upload, which typically contain your name, contact details, and work history.
  • Job descriptions. The text you paste in to evaluate or tailor against.
  • An AI API key (optional). Your Anthropic or OpenAI key, if you choose to use your own model.

Your API key is never stored

If you enter an API key, it is sent with each request from your browser and used only to make that single call to your AI provider. It is never written to the server's disk and never logged.

Where your content goes

To generate suggestions, your resume content and the job description are sent to the AI provider you select (Anthropic or OpenAI), or to a local model if you configure one. That processing is governed by your provider's own terms: Anthropic and OpenAI. Your content is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone other than the provider you choose.

Storage and retention

Resumes you upload and the PDFs the tool generates are stored on the server so the tool can function. They are tied to your API key so that, in normal use, only you see your own resumes. You can delete any resume you uploaded at any time from the Settings panel.

Want to keep everything fully private? Run the open-source version yourself, or point the tool at a local model so your data never leaves your machine.

Cookies and tracking

There are no advertising or analytics trackers. Your browser's local storage is used only to remember your settings (theme, model choice) on your own device.
